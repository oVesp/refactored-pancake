# Weapons and shields

Code: `Content/Weapons.luau` (weapon types), `Content/Items.luau` (`weapon`, `shield` fields, `offhand` slot), `Shared/Loadout.luau` (what a profile or character holds), `Shared/Archery.luau` (bow maths); server `SkillService` (basics, draw, guard rules), `CombatService` (shield guard), `StatService` (sets `Weapon` and `Shield` attributes, draw slow), `LoadoutService` (held models); client `CombatController` (attacks, aim), `CameraController` (shift-lock camera, draw zoom), `InputController`, `VfxController`. Placeholder models: `ReplicatedStorage/Weapons/*.model.json`.

Each weapon type swaps the basic attacks bound to light (Mouse 1 / pad R1) and heavy (Mouse 2 in combat mode / pad R2, touch Heavy button) attack, in the spirit of Dragon's Dogma: the same buttons, different verbs. Basic attacks stay off the GCD and cost no MP (`docs/systems/combat.md`, Timing).

| Weapon | Hands | Light | Heavy | Guard without shield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fists (nothing equipped) | One | `strike` chain (jab, cross, hook) | `heavy_strike` | Yes |
| Sword | One | `sword_slash` chain (3 cuts, wide finisher) | `sword_heavy` overhead cut | Yes |
| Bow | Two | Hold to draw, release `bow_shot` | none | No |
| Staff | Two | `staff_strike` chain (arcane melee) | `staff_bolt` charged arcane bolt | Yes |
| Wand | One | `wand_bolt` chain (3 bolts, heavier closer; 90 studs) | `wand_blast` cast arcane blast (1.2 s cast, 6 s cooldown) | No |

- **Equipment**: a weapon item names its type (`ItemDef.weapon`); shields are `kind = "Shield"` in the `offhand` slot. A two-handed weapon and a shield push each other off when equipped (`Loadout.Displaced`). `StatService.Recompute` writes `Entity.weapon`, `Entity.shieldGuard` and the character attributes `Weapon` and `Shield` (item id); `LoadoutService` welds the models from `ReplicatedStorage.Weapons` to the hand grip attachments (`Handle` part with a `Grip` attachment).
- **Who can equip**: each class lists its weapon types (`ClassDef.weapons`). A character may equip a weapon if any of its classes allows it (`Loadout.CanEquip`), so a Fighter+Mage can use a wand. Fighter allows every type but the wand; Mage and Healer allow all of them. Shields and other items are open to everyone, and fists need no item. `InventoryService` refuses a forbidden equip with the `WeaponNotAllowed` toast, and unequips the weapon (`WeaponUnequipped`) when a character loses the class that allowed it. A character with no class can equip no weapon.
- **Server rules**: `SkillService.KnownActives` holds only the equipped weapon's basics (`Loadout.Basics`) plus `shield_bash` with a shield, so a basic of another weapon is rejected as `Unknown`. The light chain check uses the weapon's own entry. `heavy = true` marks heavies that `HeavySpeed` shortens.
- **Bow**: holding heavy (aim) sends `SetDraw(true)`; light (Mouse 1) releases the shot and lowering heavy without shooting cancels the draw. Exploration mode has no Mouse 2 binding, so the bow only draws in combat mode (hotbar draws still work). The server records `drawSince` and slows the archer to `Tuning.Combat.Bow.DrawMoveMultiplier`. `CameraController.SetAiming` pushes the shoulder camera further out and narrows the FOV to 55 while drawing. The release sends `UseSkill(bow_shot)`; the server scales damage by `Archery.DrawScale` (nothing below `MinDraw`, `MinScale` → 1 by `FullDraw`, +`PerfectBonus` within `PerfectWindow` after full draw); a gold flash on the bow hand marks full draw. Arrows are free-aim projectiles with `gravity` (aim high at range), never home, and get `rearBonus` when they land within `RearArc` of the target's back. Guarding, dodging, any other skill, a stun or releasing early cancel the draw.
- **Weapon attacks**: a skill with `weaponAttack` (Fighter's Precise Attack) has no delivery or damage of its own. `Loadout.Attack` builds it from the weapon's light attack on both server and client; the server shows and reports the hit as that light attack (`shownAs`). `weaponAttack.type` and `.stat` replace the damage type and scaling stat: Precise Attack is physical and scales with PhysAtk with every weapon, staff and wand included. A hotbar skill the weapon draws starts a draw on press and shoots on release (`CombatController.ReleaseSlot`).
- **Staff and wand**: their magic is the basic attack itself (owner decision 2026-10-30): no MP, off the GCD, heavies cost stamina. Damage type `force`, scaled by `MagAtk`. The staff heavy is charged (windup). The wand trades melee for reach (owner decision 2026-10-30): its light attack is a three-bolt chain (`wand_bolt` → `wand_bolt_2` → `wand_bolt_3`, range 90) where each bolt has a windup and recovery like a swing, so the caster is slowed (`Tuning.Combat.Commit`) and locked while chaining; its heavy, `wand_blast`, is a real cast (`castTime` 1.2 s, cooldown 6 s): a cast bar, cancelled by moving before the slidecast window, stamina paid on completion, still off the GCD.
- **Shields**: while guarding (F) with a shield, its `shield` values replace `Tuning.Combat.Guard`: wider arc, longer parry window, full block (`damageMultiplier = 0`) paid in stamina per hit. Light attack while guarding uses `shield_bash`; heavy (or a light attack without a shield) drops the guard first (short shove, stun, knockback, stamina). Bow and wand cannot guard without a shield.
- **Camera and aim**: combat mode (Left Alt) is shift lock over the right shoulder; exploration mode is the normal camera; a target lock frames the target in either (`CameraController`, docs/systems/combat.md, Camera). Every weapon aims through the screen centre, never the mouse cursor, or at the locked target: projectiles fly at what the centre ray hits, melee swings where the character faces (the camera yaw).
- **Drawing and sheathing**: combat mode (Left Alt, D-pad up, Combat button) is also the weapon state. Out of combat mode a held weapon or shield is sheathed: the sword and wand hang on the hips, the shield, bow and staff on the back (`SOCKETS` in `LoadoutService`), the sword idle gives way to the default idle and walk, and `SkillService` rejects basic attacks and guard with `Sheathed`. A light, heavy or guard press while sheathed only draws (switches to combat mode) and is consumed; drawing and sheathing lock actions for `Tuning.Combat.DrawLock` (0.3 s) and there is no draw clip yet. Sheathing drops guard and a bow draw. Bare fists have nothing to draw and stay usable in both modes; spells are unaffected.
- **Sword and shield clips** (owner-authored, set `Sword` in `content/animations/sword.json`, tier final, all 1.0 s): `BasicSwordSlash1` (diagonal, `Hit` 0.68 s), `BasicSwordSlash2` (horizontal, `Hit` 0.54 s), `BasicSwordSlash3` (vertical chop, `Hit` 0.47 s) play for the `sword_slash` chain, whose windups are the `Hit` times and whose windup + recovery is the clip length (the finisher adds 0.3 s of recovery before the chain restarts). `BasicSwordIdle` replaces the default idle while a sword is held and `BasicSwordIdleNoLegs` plays instead while moving, so walk and run keep their legs (`Client/Animation/WeaponClips`). `ShieldBlock` (left arm only) replaces `GuardHold` while guarding with a shield (`GuardClips`). `sword_heavy` reuses the fist heavy clip until it has its own.
- **Placeholders**: until weapon clips exist, every other weapon basic plays the fist clips (owner decision 2026-10-30): chain steps `BasicStrike`/`BasicStrike2`/`BasicStrike3` (the wand's three bolts included, so the chain reads as a combo), heavies and the wand blast `BasicHeavyStrike`, the bow shot `BasicStrike`. Sword and staff reuse the Cleave swipe; the bow has no draw clip yet; bolts use the neon projectile, scaled by `SkillDef.projectileScale` (the wand's closing bolt is 1.6x). Weapon-specific clips and VFX are a later art pass.
- **Dev kit**: dev players (`DevService.IsDev`) get one of each basic weapon on join if their profile lacks it: `rusty_sword`, `hunting_bow`, `acolyte_staff`, `apprentice_wand`, `wooden_shield`. The grant is not checked, only equipping is, so a dev Fighter holds the wand in the inventory but needs a class that allows it to equip it.
