# VFX

Target look: the owner's reference video (2026-10-25), anime-style hits with 4 to 7 layers each. Research and plan: https://claude.ai/artifact/RbbzA7CACbBRDF4nnc7YZr

## How a hit is built

- **Layers**: a white additive core, a tinted body, sparks, filaments, a light and debris, in earth or element tones. The near-black non-additive "ink" layer behind them (it keeps a bright shape readable on daylight ground) is reserved for high-tier effects, the skills with screen effects: Charge, War Cry, Blink, Overcharge, Smite, Lay On Hands and future ultimates (owner decision 2026-10-25).
- **Timing**: the peak lasts 2 to 4 frames (about 50 ms); the rest is dissipation. Shapes appear at full size, then erode. Nothing grows slowly.
- **Allowed shapes** (owner decision 2026-10-25): textured particles, trails and beams, textured curved MeshParts (slashes, swirls, spikes) and full-screen effects (tint, flash, speed lines) on high-tier skills only. Plain Neon parts, discs and spheres stay out.
- **Feedback on contact**: authored impact, a white body flash (`Highlight`), a short light, and for the local player's own hits (given or taken) hitstop and camera shake.

## Code and data

- **Templates**: `src/ReplicatedStorage/Vfx/*.model.json`, one anchored invisible Part per effect with its emitters, attachments and Forge objects. Emitters carry VFX Forge attributes (`EmitCount`, `EmitDelay`, `EmitDuration`) and a `TextureKey` attribute naming an `Assets.Textures` key (ImageLabels carry `TextureKey` too and get their `Image` from it); the `Texture` property stays empty in the file so asset ids live only in the manifest. A camera shake is a `RayValue` tagged `CameraShake` with Forge's shake attributes. Templates are generated by `tools/vfxgen/templates.py` (`python tools/vfxgen/templates.py`), then tuned in Studio; copy tuned values back into the generator.
- **Level shapes**: particle quads always turn toward the camera (`VelocityPerpendicular` only rotates them on screen), so a shape that must lie level in the world, like the StrikeSwipe crescent, is a `SurfaceGui` on the template's top face with `ImageLabel` layers cut from a sprite sheet. The gui's `FlipbookGrid`, `FramePixels`, `Lifetime`, `Grow`, `Delay` (seconds before it starts), `Fade` (share of the life spent fading out) and `Spin` (degrees over the life) attributes and a label's optional `ColorTo` drive it in `Effects.Play`; a `FlipbookGrid` of 1 is a single image.
- **Runtime**: client `Vfx/Effects.luau` resolves template textures, starts the Forge emit module and plays a template with `Effects.Play(name, cframe)`, keeping the copy until both the Forge emit and its surfaces and lights (a `PointLight` with a `FlashTime` attribute flashes and dims) finish. `Effects.Screen(name)` plays a `ScreenGui` (speed lines) or `ColorCorrectionEffect` (tint) template for the local caster. It also owns `Hitstop` and `BodyFlash`. Skill recipes in `Vfx/SpellEffects.luau` call it (`strike`, `cleave`, `heavy_strike`, `charge`, `wolf_charge`, `war_cry`, `firebolt`, `frost_shard`, `ice_patch`, `flame_fan`); `VfxController` plays `HitImpact`, the flash, the light and, for the local player, `Hitstop` and `HitShake` when a `ShowDamage` carries a melee skill id.
- **Attached effects**: `Effects.Attach(name, part)` copies a template's continuous layers (emitters with `Rate` above 0, trails, steady `PointLight`s, attachments) onto a part such as a projectile body. A `Trail` names its end attachments with `From` and `To` attributes. `Particles.Release` stops them.
- **Charge-ups**: `Effects.Charge(name, root, duration)` holds a template at its `Offset` attribute (a `Vector3` from the root, such as the casting hand) for the cast time, its emitters ramping from 35% to full rate, then releases it with a 0.5 s linger. It returns the copy; destroying the copy cancels the charge. A recipe's `cast` returns `Instance?` for this.
- **Cones**: cone templates (`FlameFanJet`) carry per-emitter `Reach` (share of the range; speed = range / 0.45 × `Reach`) and `Arc` (share of the cone angle) attributes, which the recipe applies to the skill's range and angle.
- **Ground marks**: marks under mid-air hits (`Scorch`, `FrostSplash`) only play when the ground is within reach of the hit.
- **ForgeVFX** (`vendor/ForgeVFX/ForgeVFX.rbxm`, synced to `ReplicatedStorage.ForgeVFX`): the emit module from the owner's VFX Forge 2.4.0-rc1 plugin, extracted with `lune run tools/vendor/extract_forge <plugin .rbxm>`. Its license (VFX-DL 1.1, `vendor/ForgeVFX/LICENSE.txt`) allows it only inside the game runtime: never require it from a plugin, the command bar for anything but viewing finished effects, Lune tests or other tools. Re-extract when the plugin updates.
- **Editing in Studio**: open a template under `ReplicatedStorage.Vfx`, tune it with VFX Forge (its plugin API is `shared.vfx`, reachable from Studio MCP `execute_luau`), then write the changes back into the `.model.json` file; Rojo does not sync Studio edits to disk.
- **Textures**: originals come from `tools/vfxgen/vfxgen.py` (deterministic, project-owned, so free to upload) into `assets/original/vfx/`; see [../art/vfx-library.md](../art/vfx-library.md).
- **Skills without a template** keep the older code recipes built from `Vfx/Particles.luau` helpers.

## Templates

- `FireCharge`: fire gathering at the casting hand while Firebolt is cast (`Effects.Charge`).
- `FrostCharge`: frost gathering at the casting hand while Ice Patch is cast (`Effects.Charge`).
- `FireboltBody`: Firebolt in flight, a ragged `FireCore` head shedding flame tongues, embers and smoke (`Effects.Attach`).
- `FireboltBurst`: Firebolt hit, a hot flash, the core blooming out, flame tongues and embers flung wide, dark smoke.
- `Scorch`: an ember-glowing scorch mark that cools and fades within a second, under Firebolt hits and through the Flame Fan cone.
- `FrostShardBody`: Frost Shard in flight, a cluster of turning ice splinters in a cold glow, shedding glints and mist (`Effects.Attach`).
- `FrostBurst`: Frost Shard hit, a cold flash and ring, splinters flung along their flight, glints and mist.
- `FrostSplash`: a small patch of rime left where a frost spell lands.
- `IcePatchFloor`: the Ice Patch area, `FrostFloor` rime spreading over the ground under a cold shock ring and a low snow puff.
- `IceSpike`: one Ice Patch eruption point, splinters stabbing up out of the ground while chips fly and fall. The spikes are camera-facing `IceShard` sprites (`VelocityParallel`), not a mesh.
- `FlameFanJet`: the Flame Fan stream on a nozzle part, flame jets fanning out with per-emitter `Reach` and `Arc` (`Effects.Attach`).
