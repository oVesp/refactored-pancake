# Combat

Code: `Server/Services/SkillService.luau`, `CombatService.luau`, `StatusService.luau`, `EntityService.luau`; `Shared/Formulas.luau`, `Shared/Targeting.luau`; client `CameraController`, `TargetController`, `CombatController`, `VfxController`.

- **Entities**: players, goblins and dummies register in `EntityService` with team, level, tags and pools. Walk speed includes `entity.pace` (`StatService.SetPace`): `SetRun` from the client's double-tap W sets `Tuning.Base.Run.SpeedMultiplier`, patrolling and travelling NPCs set `Tuning.Npc.PatrolSpeedMultiplier`. It records root positions at 20 Hz for lag compensation (rewind = player ping, capped at `Tuning.Combat.MaxRewind`) and runs the movement sanity check.
- **Targeting**: `TargetController` keeps a soft enemy target, scored by `Shared/Targeting` (angle from the camera centre blended with distance, inside `Tuning.Targeting.ConeDegrees` and `Range`, with a stickiness bonus for the current one so crowds do not flicker). Tab / R3 / the touch Target button cycle a hard lock through the ranking and release it after the last; tapping an enemy on touch locks or unlocks it. A lock breaks on death, beyond `Range` or after `LockSightGrace` (1 s) out of line of sight (`Targeting.KeepLock`). The marker (`UiTemplates/TargetMarker`) is white for soft, red for locked.
- **Camera** (`CameraController`): two modes, toggled by Left Alt / D-pad up / the touch Combat button (`CameraController.ToggleCombat`, `Changed` signal, HUD label `Bind_CombatMode`); no automatic switch. Exploration (the default) is the normal Roblox camera. Combat mode draws the held weapon and shield (the server's `Drawn` attribute, set by `SetCombatMode`; sheathed they hang from body sockets, `LoadoutService`) and is shift lock: third person over the right shoulder (`Humanoid.CameraOffset` 1.75, 0.5, 0), the cursor locked at the screen centre, `AutoRotate` off and the character turned each frame to the camera's yaw (not while seated, climbing or dead). Roblox's own Shift toggle is off (`StarterPlayer.EnableMouseLockOption = false`). A hard target lock works in both modes: the shoulder offset comes on, the camera turns around its focus toward the target (pitch kept within a small range) and the character faces it; releasing the lock returns to the mode's own framing. Any open modal, world menu or the Roblox menu releases everything (free cursor, `AutoRotate` on, no offset) until it closes. Drawing the bow calls `SetAiming`: offset 2, 0.6, 0 and FOV 55, character facing the camera. Weapon attacks and guard need combat mode when a weapon or shield is held (a press while sheathed draws it first; docs/systems/weapons.md, Drawing and sheathing); bare fists and spells work in both.
- **Aim per delivery** (`CombatController`): the aim point is what the ray through the screen centre hits (starting level with the character, so nothing between the camera and it counts), else a point 280 studs along it; the mouse cursor never steers an attack. Melee/Cone swing where the character faces (the camera yaw) and turn toward a target within skill range + `MeleeAssistRange` and `MeleeAssistDegrees` of that facing (any angle when locked). Projectile is free-aim through the centre; a hard lock sends it at the locked target, and off the mouse it snaps to the soft target within `AimAssistDegrees`. Target and Dash need an enemy target, else the client shows "No target" and sends nothing. Ground lands on the centre point, or on the target when locked or off the mouse. Ally sends the ally at the screen centre, else the wounded ally nearest the centre, else none (self).
- **Timing** (`Tuning.Combat.Gcd`): skills with `gcd = true` (every spell, Cleave) start and wait on the shared 2.5 s GCD, plus their own `cooldown` when they have one (Flame Fan, Ice Patch, Arcane Shield, Shelter, Bless). Other class actives (Charge, War Cry, Blink, Overcharge, Rebuke, Lay On Hands) are off-GCD with their own `cooldown`. Every non-basic player action sets an animation lock of `AnimationLock` (or the cast time, if longer) that blocks all skills, basics included; basic attacks never set it and stay off the GCD, except a basic with a `castTime` (the wand blast), which locks for its cast. Melee commitment (`Tuning.Combat.Commit`): basic attacks carry `windup` (press to hit), `recovery` (after the hit) and `lunge`. The server resolves the hit when the windup ends and locks the player until the recovery ends (`lockedUntil`), slowed to `MoveMultiplier` of normal speed (`Entity.commitUntil`, applied in `StatService.Recompute`); a stun, or a dodge in the windup, cancels the swing and refunds it, and a dodge in the recovery clears the lock. Stamina cost, cooldown (equal to windup + recovery) and hit checks use the existing path. The client predicts the lock, steps `lunge` studs over `LungeSeconds` toward the aim (stopping short of it) and buffers one press made within `QueueWindow` of the lock ending; enemy skills do not use these fields; their basic melee swings are paced to the 1 s sword swing instead (windup 0.6 to 0.8 s, cooldown 1.8 to 2.5 s; the brute slam and chieftain stomp stay slower). The light attack is a chain (`Shared/Chain`): `strike` → `strike_2` → `strike_3`, linked by `SkillDef.chainNext` (a hit with `chainWindow`, such as the sword slashes whose 1 s cooldown outlasts the default window, uses it instead of `Tuning.Combat.Chain.Window`). A light press uses the last hit's `chainNext` when it comes within `Tuning.Combat.Chain.Window` (0.8 s) of that hit, otherwise `strike`; each hit also waits out the previous hit's cooldown, so the finisher's 0.8 s cooldown is the recovery before the chain starts again. The client predicts the next hit; the server accepts `strike` at any time and a later hit only right after its predecessor within the window (plus 0.15 s jitter slack), else `RejectSkill(seq, "Chain")`, which also resets the client's chain. Only the `Haste` extra (item affixes, `Formulas.Hasted`, capped at `MaxHaste`) shortens the GCD and cast times. The client predicts the GCD, cooldowns and lock, queues one hotbar press made within `QueueWindow` of readiness and sends it when ready, and rolls everything back on `RejectSkill` or an `Interrupted` cast. The hotbar sweep shows the longer of the GCD and the skill's own cooldown.
- **Weapons**: the equipped weapon (`Content/Weapons`, character attribute `Weapon`) decides which basic attacks light and heavy attack use, and whether guarding needs a shield; see `docs/systems/weapons.md`. The fist chain below is the unarmed case.
- **Use**: the client sends `UseSkill(skillId, aim, seq, target?)`. The server checks alive, known, not disabled, not casting or locked, cooldown and GCD, aim sanity, the target (Target/Dash: alive enemy in range with rewind, else `NoTarget`/`Range`; Ally: an invalid ally falls back to self) and cost (instant skills pay now; cast-time skills only check, and pay when the cast completes), then calls `SkillService.Cast(caster, skillId, aim, target, cost?)`. Rejections return `RejectSkill(seq, reason)`; the client clears its predicted cooldown. NPCs pass their target the same way.
- **Chat casting**: a chat message that is exactly the name of a known spell or dev skill (`Shared/ChatCast`: case, accents, punctuation and extra spaces ignored; `chatArg` skills such as Gate take the rest of the message as an argument) casts it. The client's `CombatController` hooks `TextChatService.SendingMessage`, resolves the name against its snapshot, applies the usual prediction and sends `CastSpellByName(text, aim, seq, target?)`; `ChatCastService` resolves the name again against the server's known list and runs `SkillService.Use`, the same checks as `UseSkill` (GCD, cast time, cost, cooldown, target). Each text channel's `ShouldDeliverCallback` drops a message that resolves to a castable spell for its sender; every other message, and `/` commands, stay ordinary chat. Class actives and basic attacks do not cast from chat.
- **Cast**: windup or cast time (cast time shortened by `Haste`; Heavy Strike windup by `HeavySpeed`) is broadcast as `ShowCast` (Cast or Telegraph). Stun, fear, knockback and dodging interrupt it; a player (or an interruptible NPC cast) that drifts more than `MoveTolerance` studs before the last `SlidecastWindow` seconds is interrupted too. An interrupt broadcasts `Interrupted`, refunds the GCD, cooldown and lock, and nothing is paid. The HUD cast bar turns green in the slidecast window.
- **Visuals**: projectiles broadcast `ShowCast` (Projectile, with `homing` set to the target model for Target spells) and `ShowImpact`; every other delivery broadcasts `ShowCast` (Resolve, with the chosen ally as `target`) when it resolves. The client draws them in `VfxController` (recipes in `Vfx/SpellEffects` and templates played by `Vfx/Effects`, see `docs/systems/vfx.md`); `ShowDamage` carries the skill id and source model, and melee hits play the authored impact, body flash and, for the local player, hitstop and camera shake; the local player's light swing is predicted at the clip's `Hit` marker, so its Resolve is skipped; telegraphed swings (Heavy Strike) are not predicted and draw on Resolve.
- **Animation**: a skill's `animation` names an `Animation` under ReplicatedStorage.Animations (`Animation/Animations`, docs/art/animation-pipeline.md; the marker `Hit` is the effect frame; the dodge plays `Dodge`). `VfxController` loads the local character's animations at spawn and plays one with Action priority when `CombatController.Used` fires, stopping it on `RejectSkill` or an `Interrupted` cast. Basic clips (`BasicStrike` 0.4 s jab, `BasicStrike2` 0.4 s cross with the other hand, `BasicStrike3` 0.6 s rising hook with a visible recovery, `BasicHeavyStrike` 1.0 s; uploaded, re-upload edits with `tools/anim/anim.py`, see `docs/art/animation-pipeline.md`) move the upper body only, so walk and run keep playing. Light chain `Hit` markers sit at 0.133 s, 0.133 s and 0.233 s. Guard clips play from `Animation/GuardAnimations`: `GuardHold` (forearms in front of the face) loops with Action priority while the character's `Guarding` attribute is true, and `GuardParry` (0.33 s outward deflection, `Parry` marker at 0.033 s) plays at Action2 when `ShowParry` names the local character as defender. Clips are posed and baked headless by `tools/blender/combat_poses.py` and `combat_bake.py`; previews live in `assets/blender/combat_r15/preview/`. Markers are cosmetic: the light swing VFX and sound wait for `Hit` (light attacks have `telegraph = false`: no ground telegraph, and the owner's client draws the swing at the marker); Heavy Strike's swing still comes from the server Resolve at the end of its windup, which its `Hit` marker (0.5 s) matches.
- **Deliveries**: Melee/Cone (arc against rewound positions), SelfArea, Ground (clamped to range), Projectile (server-stepped segment test that stops on walls, hits the first enemy), Target (homing bolt that chases one target, re-validated when the cast ends, stops on walls, gives up after `HomingRangeFactor` × range), Ally (the chosen ally, else self), Self, Dash (to the chosen enemy), Blink (wall-checked), Object (tagged world props), Summon (raises the chosen summon; [AI and spawning](ai-and-spawning.md), Summons). AoE deliveries have no target cap.
- **Damage**: `CombatService.DealDamage` calls `Formulas.ResolveDamage` (type tags, crit, level gap, defense). Guarding while facing the attacker parries inside `ParryWindow` (attacker stunned, `ShowDamage` kind `parried` plus `ShowParry(defender, attacker)` to every client), otherwise takes 40% damage and costs stamina (`ShowDamage` kind `blocked`). Dodge grants i-frames. Shields absorb first. Players in safe zones cannot be harmed; the same team never.
- **Statuses**: data in `Content/Statuses`. Dot/Hot ticks go through `CombatService`, so every HP change shares one path. Harmful durations shrink with Resistance; immune tags block statuses by id.
