# Combat

Code: `Server/Services/SkillService.luau`, `CombatService.luau`, `StatusService.luau`, `EntityService.luau`; `Shared/Formulas.luau`, `Shared/Targeting.luau`; client `TargetController`, `CombatController`, `VfxController`.

- **Entities**: players, goblins and dummies register in `EntityService` with team, level, tags and pools. It records root positions at 20 Hz for lag compensation (rewind = player ping, capped at `Tuning.Combat.MaxRewind`) and runs the movement sanity check.
- **Targeting**: `TargetController` keeps a soft enemy target, scored by `Shared/Targeting` (angle from the camera centre blended with distance, inside `Tuning.Targeting.ConeDegrees` and `Range`, with a stickiness bonus for the current one so crowds do not flicker). Tab / R3 / the touch Target button cycle a hard lock through the ranking and release it after the last; tapping an enemy on touch locks or unlocks it. A lock breaks on death or beyond `Range`. The marker (`UiTemplates/TargetMarker`) is white for soft, red for locked.
- **Aim per delivery** (`CombatController`): Melee/Cone turn toward a target within skill range + `MeleeAssistRange` and `MeleeAssistDegrees` of the aim (any angle when locked). Projectile is free-aim; off the mouse it snaps to the target within `AimAssistDegrees` (or when locked). Target and Dash need an enemy target, else the client shows "No target" and sends nothing. Ground uses the cursor, or the target off the mouse. Ally sends the ally under the mouse, else the wounded ally nearest the centre, else none (self).
- **Timing** (`Tuning.Combat.Gcd`): skills with `gcd = true` (every spell, Cleave) start and wait on the shared 2.5 s GCD, plus their own `cooldown` when they have one (Flame Fan, Ice Patch, Arcane Shield, Shelter, Bless). Other class actives (Charge, War Cry, Blink, Overcharge, Rebuke, Lay On Hands) are off-GCD with their own `cooldown`. Every non-basic player action sets an animation lock of `AnimationLock` (or the cast time, if longer) that blocks all skills, basics included; basic attacks never set it and stay off the GCD. Only the `Haste` extra (item affixes, `Formulas.Hasted`, capped at `MaxHaste`) shortens the GCD and cast times. The client predicts the GCD, cooldowns and lock, queues one hotbar press made within `QueueWindow` of readiness and sends it when ready, and rolls everything back on `RejectSkill` or an `Interrupted` cast. The hotbar sweep shows the longer of the GCD and the skill's own cooldown.
- **Use**: the client sends `UseSkill(skillId, aim, seq, target?)`. The server checks alive, known, not disabled, not casting or locked, cooldown and GCD, aim sanity, the target (Target/Dash: alive enemy in range with rewind, else `NoTarget`/`Range`; Ally: an invalid ally falls back to self) and cost (instant skills pay now; cast-time skills only check, and pay when the cast completes), then calls `SkillService.Cast(caster, skillId, aim, target, cost?)`. Rejections return `RejectSkill(seq, reason)`; the client clears its predicted cooldown. NPCs pass their target the same way.
- **Cast**: windup or cast time (cast time shortened by `Haste`; Heavy Strike windup by `HeavySpeed`) is broadcast as `ShowCast` (Cast or Telegraph). Stun, fear, knockback and dodging interrupt it; a player (or an interruptible NPC cast) that drifts more than `MoveTolerance` studs before the last `SlidecastWindow` seconds is interrupted too. An interrupt broadcasts `Interrupted`, refunds the GCD, cooldown and lock, and nothing is paid. The HUD cast bar turns green in the slidecast window.
- **Visuals**: projectiles broadcast `ShowCast` (Projectile, with `homing` set to the target model for Target spells) and `ShowImpact`; every other delivery broadcasts `ShowCast` (Resolve, with the chosen ally as `target`) when it resolves. The client draws them in `VfxController` (recipes in `Vfx/SpellEffects`, see `docs/art/vfx-library.md`); the local player's instant melee swing is predicted, so its Resolve is skipped.
- **Deliveries**: Melee/Cone (arc against rewound positions), SelfArea, Ground (clamped to range), Projectile (server-stepped segment test that stops on walls, hits the first enemy), Target (homing bolt that chases one target, re-validated when the cast ends, stops on walls, gives up after `HomingRangeFactor` × range), Ally (the chosen ally, else self), Self, Dash (to the chosen enemy), Blink (wall-checked), Object (tagged world props). AoE deliveries have no target cap.
- **Damage**: `CombatService.DealDamage` calls `Formulas.ResolveDamage` (type tags, crit, level gap, defense). Guarding while facing the attacker parries inside `ParryWindow` (attacker stunned), otherwise takes 40% damage and costs stamina. Dodge grants i-frames. Shields absorb first. Players in safe zones cannot be harmed; the same team never.
- **Statuses**: data in `Content/Statuses`. Dot/Hot ticks go through `CombatService`, so every HP change shares one path. Harmful durations shrink with Resistance; immune tags block statuses by id.
