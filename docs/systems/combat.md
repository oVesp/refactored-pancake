# Combat

Code: `Server/Services/SkillService.luau`, `CombatService.luau`, `StatusService.luau`, `EntityService.luau`; `Shared/Formulas.luau`, `Shared/Targeting.luau`, `Shared/Casting.luau`; client `CameraController`, `TargetController`, `CombatController`, `VfxController`.

- **Entities**: players, goblins and dummies register in `EntityService` with team, level, tags and pools. Walk speed includes `entity.pace` (`StatService.SetPace`): `SetRun` from the client's double-tap W sets `Tuning.Base.Run.SpeedMultiplier`, patrolling and travelling NPCs set `Tuning.Npc.PatrolSpeedMultiplier`. It records root positions at 20 Hz for lag compensation (rewind = player ping, capped at `Tuning.Combat.MaxRewind`) and runs the movement sanity check.
- **Targeting**: `TargetController` keeps a soft enemy target, scored by `Shared/Targeting` (angle from the camera centre blended with distance, inside `Tuning.Targeting.ConeDegrees` and `Range`, with a stickiness bonus for the current one so crowds do not flicker). Tab / R3 / the touch Target button cycle a hard lock through the ranking and release it after the last; tapping an enemy on touch locks or unlocks it. A lock breaks on death, beyond `Range` or after `LockSightGrace` (1 s) out of line of sight (`Targeting.KeepLock`). The marker (`UiTemplates/TargetMarker`) is white for soft, red for locked.
- **Camera** (`CameraController`): two modes, toggled by Left Alt / D-pad up / the touch Combat button (`CameraController.ToggleCombat`, `Changed` signal, HUD label `Bind_CombatMode`); no automatic switch. Exploration (the default) is the normal Roblox camera. Combat mode draws the held weapon and shield (the server's `Drawn` attribute, set by `SetCombatMode`; sheathed they hang from body sockets, `LoadoutService`) and is shift lock: third person over the right shoulder (`Humanoid.CameraOffset` 1.75, 0.5, 0), the cursor locked at the screen centre, `AutoRotate` off and the character turned each frame to the camera's yaw (not while seated, climbing or dead). Roblox's own Shift toggle is off (`StarterPlayer.EnableMouseLockOption = false`). A hard target lock works in both modes: the shoulder offset comes on, the camera turns around its focus toward the target (pitch kept within a small range) and the character faces it; releasing the lock returns to the mode's own framing. Any open modal, world menu or the Roblox menu releases everything (free cursor, `AutoRotate` on, no offset) until it closes. Drawing the bow calls `SetAiming`: offset 2, 0.6, 0 and FOV 55, character facing the camera. Weapon attacks and guard need combat mode when a weapon or shield is held (a press while sheathed draws it first; docs/systems/weapons.md, Drawing and sheathing); bare fists and spells work in both.
- **Aim per delivery** (`CombatController`): the aim point is what the ray through the screen centre hits (starting level with the character, so nothing between the camera and it counts), else a point 280 studs along it; the mouse cursor never steers an attack. Melee/Cone swing where the character faces (the camera yaw) and turn toward a target within skill range + `MeleeAssistRange` and `MeleeAssistDegrees` of that facing (any angle when locked). Projectile is free-aim through the centre; a hard lock sends it at the locked target, and off the mouse it snaps to the soft target within `AimAssistDegrees`. Target and Dash need an enemy target, else the client shows "No target" and sends nothing (a held spell asks at its release, so the caster may charge first and find the target after). Ground lands on the centre point, or on the target when locked or off the mouse. Ally sends the ally at the screen centre, else the wounded ally nearest the centre, else none (self).
- **Timing** (`Tuning.Combat.Gcd`): skills with `gcd = true` (every spell, Cleave) start at the press and wait on the shared 2.5 s GCD, plus their own `cooldown` when they have one (Flame Fan, Ice Patch, Arcane Shield, Shelter, Bless). Other class actives (Charge, War Cry, Blink, Overcharge, Rebuke, Lay On Hands) are off-GCD with their own `cooldown`. Every non-basic player action sets an animation lock of `AnimationLock` that blocks all skills, basics included (a spell locks for its whole charge and `AnimationLock` after it fires); basic attacks never set it and stay off the GCD. Melee commitment (`Tuning.Combat.Commit`): basic attacks carry `windup` (press to hit), `recovery` (after the hit) and `lunge`. The server resolves the hit when the windup ends and locks the player until the recovery ends (`lockedUntil`), slowed to `MoveMultiplier` of normal speed (`Entity.commitUntil`, applied in `StatService.Recompute`); a stun, or a dodge in the windup, cancels the swing and refunds it, and a dodge in the recovery clears the lock. Stamina cost, cooldown (equal to windup + recovery) and hit checks use the existing path. The client predicts the lock, steps `lunge` studs over `LungeSeconds` toward the aim (stopping short of it) and buffers one press made within `QueueWindow` of the lock ending; enemy skills do not use these fields; their basic melee swings are paced to the 1 s sword swing instead (windup 0.6 to 0.8 s, cooldown 1.8 to 2.5 s; the brute slam and chieftain stomp stay slower). The light attack is a chain (`Shared/Chain`): `strike` → `strike_2` → `strike_3`, linked by `SkillDef.chainNext` (a hit with `chainWindow`, such as the sword slashes whose 1 s cooldown outlasts the default window, uses it instead of `Tuning.Combat.Chain.Window`). A light press uses the last hit's `chainNext` when it comes within `Tuning.Combat.Chain.Window` (0.8 s) of that hit, otherwise `strike`; each hit also waits out the previous hit's cooldown, so the finisher's 0.8 s cooldown is the recovery before the chain starts again. The client predicts the next hit; the server accepts `strike` at any time and a later hit only right after its predecessor within the window (plus 0.15 s jitter slack), else `RejectSkill(seq, "Chain")`, which also resets the client's chain. Only the `Haste` extra (item affixes, `Formulas.Hasted`, capped at `MaxHaste`) shortens the GCD and cast times. The client predicts the GCD, cooldowns and lock, queues one hotbar press made within `QueueWindow` of readiness and sends it when ready, and rolls everything back on `RejectSkill` or an `Interrupted` cast. The hotbar sweep shows the longer of the GCD and the skill's own cooldown.
- **Weapons**: the equipped weapon (`Content/Weapons`, character attribute `Weapon`) decides which basic attacks light and heavy attack use, and whether guarding needs a shield; see `docs/systems/weapons.md`. The fist chain below is the unarmed case.
- **Use**: the client sends `UseSkill(skillId, aim, seq, target?)`. The server checks alive, known, not disabled, not casting or locked, cooldown and GCD, aim sanity, the target (Target/Dash: alive enemy in range with rewind, else `NoTarget`/`Range`; Ally: an invalid ally falls back to self) and cost (instant skills pay now; spells only check, and pay when they fire), then calls `SkillService.Cast(caster, skillId, aim, target, cost?, holdSeq?)`. A held spell skips the target check here; it happens at the release. Rejections return `RejectSkill(seq, reason)`; the client clears its predicted cooldown. NPCs pass their target the same way.
- **Chat casting**: a chat message that is exactly the name of a known spell or dev skill (`Shared/ChatCast`: case, accents, punctuation and extra spaces ignored; `chatArg` skills such as Gate take the rest of the message as an argument) casts it. The client's `CombatController` hooks `TextChatService.SendingMessage`, resolves the name against its snapshot, applies the usual prediction and sends `CastSpellByName(text, aim, seq, target?)`; `ChatCastService` resolves the name again against the server's known list and runs `SkillService.Use`, the same checks as `UseSkill` (GCD, cost, cooldown, target). Chat has no button to let go, so a chat-cast spell needs its target at once, locks the caster the same and fires when full. A held weapon's spell casts from chat too. Each text channel's `ShouldDeliverCallback` drops a message that resolves to a castable spell for its sender; every other message, and `/` commands, stay ordinary chat. Class actives and basic attacks do not cast from chat.
- **Cast** (`Shared/Casting`, `Tuning.Combat.Cast`): windup or cast time (cast time shortened by `Haste`; Heavy Strike windup by `HeavySpeed`) is broadcast as `ShowCast` (Cast or Telegraph, with `hold` on a held spell). Stun, fear, knockback and dodging interrupt it.
  - **Spells, Dragon's Dogma style**: every spell has a `castTime`; the only player skills that do. Pressing starts the charge: the server sets `Entity.casting` with `locked`, which slows the caster to `MoveMultiplier` (0.2) of normal speed in `StatService.Recompute` and blocks every other action but dodge. Holding past full keeps the spell ready (the client bursts gold at the hand, the HUD bar stays full and green, the charge effect keeps playing). Letting go sends `ReleaseCast(seq, aim, target?)` with the current aim and target, and the server decides from its own clock (`Casting.Release`): full fires it now; within `ReleaseGrace` (0.12 s) short of full, which absorbs network jitter, it fires the moment it fills; any earlier **fizzles**: an interrupt with a full refund, as in Dragon's Dogma, so a tapped spell does nothing. The client cannot shorten a charge: only the server's timer fills it, and a release must name the press's `seq`. The target is settled at the release: no valid target rejects and cancels. A full charge held `MaxHold` (10 s) cancels, so a lost release cannot lock anyone for good. `AnimationLock` follows the fire.
  - **Utility spells** (`SkillDef.utility`: Light, Kindle, Glow, Purify Water, Create Water, Quick March) charge in `UtilityMax` (0.5 s) or less; every other spell in `SpellMin` (1 s) or more (`Content.spec`). Their flow is the same.
  - **No button to let go**: chat casting and the summon picker (a press with `arg`) fire when full; NPC casts always do. They need their target at the start.
  - **Movement** (`Casting.Step`): the first `MoveGrace` (0.25 s) does not count, since the caster is still slowing from before the press; the player's ping is added to that grace, since the creep reaches their client a round trip late; the caster is then anchored and is interrupted past `MoveTolerance` (2.5) flat studs from there. At the creep speed a tapped key stays inside it and about a second of sustained walking crosses it. The same check applies to interruptible NPC casts. **Dodging** cancels at once, on the press, even if the roll itself is refused. Sheathing cancels a weapon's spell.
  - **Hits** (`Poise.CastHit`, `Tuning.Combat.Poise`): a hit of at least `CastPoise` (15%) × max HP poise damage interrupts a spell, charging or full; a lighter one pushes a charge that is not yet full back by `Pushback` (0.25 s), at most `PushbackMax` (0.75 s) per cast, sending `ShowCast` kind `Pushback` (with `remaining`) to move the HUD bar and the client's prediction. Melee windups are not pushed back. AI disruptors count on a held full charge lasting to its `holdUntil` (`Casting.Remaining`).
  - An interrupt broadcasts `Interrupted`, refunds the GCD, cooldown and lock, and nothing is paid.
- **Visuals**: projectiles broadcast `ShowCast` (Projectile, with `homing` set to the target model for Target spells) and `ShowImpact`; every other delivery broadcasts `ShowCast` (Resolve, with the chosen ally as `target`) when it resolves. The client draws them in `VfxController` (recipes in `Vfx/SpellEffects` and templates played by `Vfx/Effects`, see `docs/systems/vfx.md`); `ShowDamage` carries the skill id and source model, and melee hits play the authored impact, body flash and, for the local player, hitstop (× the skill's `poiseDamage` factor, 1 to `Tuning.Swarm.Poise.HitstopMaxScale`; Heavy Strike and Overhead Cut carry 1.5) and camera shake; the local player's light swing is predicted at the clip's `Hit` marker, so its Resolve is skipped; telegraphed swings (Heavy Strike) are not predicted and draw on Resolve.
- **Animation**: a skill's `animation` names an `Animation` under ReplicatedStorage.Animations (`Animation/Animations`, docs/art/animation-pipeline.md; the marker `Hit` is the effect frame; the dodge plays `Dodge`). `VfxController` loads the local character's animations at spawn and plays one with Action priority when `CombatController.Used` fires (a spell's when it fires, not at the press; there is no charge clip yet, so the charge shows through its effect and the HUD bar), stopping it on `RejectSkill` or an `Interrupted` cast. Basic clips (`BasicStrike` 0.4 s jab, `BasicStrike2` 0.4 s cross with the other hand, `BasicStrike3` 0.6 s rising hook with a visible recovery, `BasicHeavyStrike` 1.0 s; uploaded, re-upload edits with `tools/anim/anim.py`, see `docs/art/animation-pipeline.md`) move the upper body only, so walk and run keep playing. Light chain `Hit` markers sit at 0.133 s, 0.133 s and 0.233 s. Guard clips play from `Animation/GuardAnimations`: `GuardHold` (forearms in front of the face) loops with Action priority while the character's `Guarding` attribute is true, and `GuardParry` (0.33 s outward deflection, `Parry` marker at 0.033 s) plays at Action2 when `ShowParry` names the local character as defender. Clips are posed and baked headless by `tools/blender/combat_poses.py` and `combat_bake.py`; previews live in `assets/blender/combat_r15/preview/`. Markers are cosmetic: the light swing VFX and sound wait for `Hit` (light attacks have `telegraph = false`: no ground telegraph, and the owner's client draws the swing at the marker); Heavy Strike's swing still comes from the server Resolve at the end of its windup, which its `Hit` marker (0.5 s) matches.
- **Deliveries**: Melee/Cone (arc against rewound positions), SelfArea, Ground (clamped to range), Projectile (server-stepped segment test that stops on walls, hits the first enemy), Target (homing bolt that chases one target, re-validated when the cast ends, stops on walls, gives up after `HomingRangeFactor` × range), Ally (the chosen ally, else self), Self, Dash (to the chosen enemy), Blink (wall-checked), Object (tagged world props), Summon (raises the chosen summon; [AI and spawning](ai-and-spawning.md), Summons). AoE deliveries have no target cap.
- **Damage**: `CombatService.DealDamage` calls `Formulas.ResolveDamage` (type tags, crit, level gap, defense). Guarding while facing the attacker parries inside `ParryWindow` when the hit's parry class allows it (below; `ShowDamage` kind `parried` plus `ShowParry(defender, attacker)` to every client, no damage and none of the hit's effects), otherwise takes 40% damage and costs stamina (`ShowDamage` kind `blocked`). A dodged hit also carries none of its effects. Dodge is a committed roll (`Tuning.Combat.Dodge`: 0.8 s, i-frames for the first 0.4 s, 11.25 studs on a slowing curve, facing the roll direction whatever the camera or shift lock does peaking near walking speed, locked from other actions until it ends, 0.15 s cooldown after); the `Dodge` animation is retimed to the roll's duration. Shields absorb first. Players in safe zones cannot be harmed; the same team never.
- **Poise and parry** (`Shared/Poise`, `Tuning.Swarm.Poise`, `Tuning.Parry`): every NPC has a poise pool, `combat.poiseMax` at its base stats (default `DefaultShare` 0.2 × base MaxHp), growing with max HP by level (`Poise.Pool`). A hit takes `damage × poiseDamage` (skill field, default `DamageFactor` 1) from it (`CombatService.PoiseDamaged`); damage over time takes none. The pool refills after `RefillDelay` (3 s) without poise damage. An empty pool breaks: the windup is cancelled, the token goes back and the NPC stops deciding for `Seconds` (0.6 s); no new break, and no poise damage, for `Cooldown` (3 s). Parry classes (`SkillDef.parry`, default Normal for Melee, Cone and Dash, else None): Normal parries inside the window; Heavy only in its first `HeavyWindowShare` (half) unless the defender is the higher level; None (projectiles, areas) is only blocked. A parry (`CombatService.Parried`) takes `combat.parryPoise` (default 1) of the attacker's full pool × clamp(1 + 0.1 × (defender level − attacker level), 0.25, 2). So same-level Rabble break on one parry; brutes, soldiers and riders (0.95) break on a second parry within the refill delay or on one from a higher-level defender; the Captain and Chieftain (0) never break by parry. A break staggers the attacker for `BreakSeconds` (1.5 s) and opens a riposte window as long: the first melee hit it takes deals `RiposteMultiplier` (1.5×). A parry that does not break, or lands in the stagger cooldown, makes it flinch for `FlinchSeconds` (0.2 s). Feedback: the server sets `AnimCue` `Parried` on a break (sets without a `Parried` clip play `Hit`) and `Hit` on a flinch, and fires `ShowBreak(model, source, "Break" | "Flinch")`, also for breaks by hits; `VfxController` flashes the NPC gold, bursts on a break and plays `BreakHitstop` or `FlinchHitstop` with a shake on a break when the local player is involved. Parry breaks log at debug level.
- **Threat** (`Shared/Threat`, `Tuning.Threat`): a base mechanic every entity generates and every NPC reads; class skills only supply data. A hit adds `damage × skill threat.multiplier × source multiplier` to the victim's `threat[source]`, and a skill's `threat.flat` adds a fixed amount per enemy it hits (War Cry: 40, on top of its taunt). The source multiplier is the entity's `threatMultiplier` (default 1) times the `threatMultiplier` of each status it carries. Healing and shielding add `restored × HealThreat` (0.5), split across every enemy whose threat table holds the healed ally; Hot ticks count. The first entry in an NPC's table, from the first hit or from being the nearest in its aggro radius, also carries `PullThreat` (10), so whoever engages first holds the pack; squad alerts and allies pulled in by damage take the same opening. Every think an NPC's threat on targets beyond its longest attack reach plus `ReachSlack` (6) decays by `DecayPerSecond` (5%). Target choice: a taunt forces the taunter; otherwise targets in reach beat higher threat out of reach, except one casting a heal or shield; among those the highest threat wins, but the current target holds until a challenger exceeds its threat by `SwitchRatio` (1.1 for Melee-slot NPCs, 1.3 for the rest). Returning home clears the table. Aggro switches log at debug level.
- **Statuses**: data in `Content/Statuses`. Dot/Hot ticks go through `CombatService`, so every HP change shares one path. Harmful durations shrink with Resistance; immune tags block statuses by id. A stunned player refuses new Stun-kind statuses (stun, fear, sleep), and so does a player whose stun ended less than `Tuning.Combat.Poise.StunGuard` (1.5 s) ago, so a pack cannot chain-stun anyone.
