# AI and spawning

Code: `Server/Services/AIService.luau`, `SquadService.luau`, `SpawnService.luau`, `Shared/Swarm.luau`, `Client/Controllers/EnemyAnimationController.luau`; template `src/ServerStorage/Templates/Goblin.rbxmx`.

Template: skinned `Body` MeshPart (bones from `tools/world/goblin_rig.json`) driven by a Humanoid and `HumanoidRootPart`, plus an invisible `Head` anchor for nameplates. Spawn scales it by `def.scale × BaseScale` (1.5) and tints `Body`; the SurfaceAppearance leaves the skin transparent so the colour shows. The model streams atomically.

Animation: AIService sets `AnimCue = "<clip>:<seq>"` on attack (melee, dash and self-area skills) or throw (everything else), hit and death. The client plays locomotion from root speed (idle, walk, run above 10 studs/s) and one-shots from the cue. Clip ids come from `Assets.Animations`; entries without an uploaded id fall back to the KeyframeSequence in `ReplicatedStorage.Animations`, which only resolves in Studio.

- **Spawners** are invisible parts tagged `Spawner` (`EnemyId`, `Count`, `LevelMin`, `LevelMax`, `RespawnSeconds`, `Radius`). Active cap per zone: `Tuning.Npc.ActivePerZone`. Dead goblins despawn after 3 s; their spawner refills after its timer.
- **Brains** think every 0.25 s with jitter: Idle → Patrol (random point near home) → Aggro (taunt, then threat, then the nearest player in aggro radius) → Position (waiting for an attack token) → Disrupt (running at a casting player) → Flee / Regroup (morale broken) → Stagger (poise broken) → Attack (first ready skill in data order whose conditions hold and whose budget grants a token) → Return (beyond the leash: clear threat, walk home, regenerate, reset). Players in safe zones are never targeted. Damage alerts nearby idle enemies of the same team.
- **Behaviors** come from data: `keepDistance` makes ranged units kite; Ally skills heal the most wounded ally; Dash skills open from range; group behaviour comes from `combat` (below). `EnemyDef.behavior` is kept only for special cases: `Dummy` never thinks, `Boss` labels an encounter boss (no AI branch). `combat.summons` spawns adds once HP falls below each entry's `belowHp`, applies its `selfEffects` and sets the `Phase` attribute (the Chieftain: two level-4 Raiders and Rally at 50%).
- **Movement** uses `Humanoid:MoveTo` on clear lines and `PathfindingService` (pcall, staggered recompute) when blocked.
- `AiState` is replicated as an attribute for the debug overlay. In Studio, brains also publish `AiRole`, `AiSlot` (ring, index and Front/Side/Rear), `AiToken` (`Held` or `Spent`) and `AiMorale` every think.

## Group combat

One general system for every enemy, configured per enemy in `EnemyDef.combat` (`Content/Types`) with defaults in `Tuning.Swarm`. Pure logic lives in `Shared/Swarm.luau` (unit-tested under Lune); `SquadService` owns the live state; `AIService` asks it before acting.

- **Squads.** Enemies attacking the same player form that player's squad. The attack budget belongs to the player, so goblins and undead on one player share it without cooperating.
- **Attack tokens.** Each player target carries its own budget, `Tuning.Swarm.Budget` (2 Melee + 1 Ranged, plus 1 Support for ally skills), so the total grows with the number of players in a fight. A skill draws from the budget of its kind (`Swarm.TokenKind`: ally skills Support; Melee, Dash and SelfArea Melee; the rest Ranged), not from the enemy's slot, so an enemy with a heal and a strike uses both. `combat.tokenCost` (default 1, large enemies 2) is the share one attack takes. Slot `"None"` never waits (training dummy).
- **Token lifecycle.** A melee enemy queues for a token as soon as an attack skill is ready, before walking into reach; without one it holds `WaitDistance` studs beyond its reach in the `Position` state, facing the target (ranged enemies wait at their own distance). Queues are per kind and FIFO by when waiting began, so the enemy that just attacked goes to the back. Spending a token sets its release to the end of the windup or cast plus `RecoveryHold`; an unused grant is taken back after `TokenTimeout`; a waiter that stops asking for `WaitStale` loses its place. Interrupts, death, leaving the target and returning home give the token back at once.
- **Engagement slots.** A tactical update per target, every `TacticInterval` (0.5 s) with a random start offset so targets do not update together, spreads attackers over two rings around the player: `Slots.Melee` (6) for melee enemies, `Slots.Ranged` (8) for everyone else. A slot is a bearing; each enemy stands on it at its own reach (melee: its shortest attack reach, capped at 8) (melee with a token at reach − 1, without one at reach + `WaitDistance`). `Swarm.AssignSlots` is greedy: it keeps taking the cheapest (enemy, free slot) pair, where cost is the walk to the slot plus `SlotSwitchCost` for leaving the current slot plus `SlotCongestion` for every taken neighbouring bearing in either ring. No slot is shared; enemies left over approach directly. Slots whose ray from the player hits a wall within the ring's widest radius are skipped.
- **Separation.** Enemies closer than `Separation.Radius` (4 studs) push each other apart, up to `Separation.Strength` studs added to the slot destination (`Swarm.Separation`).
- **Roles.** Each team on a target is its own squad. Every tactical update gives each member one role from its `combat.roles` weights (default `{ Pressure = 1 }`), capped per squad by `RoleCaps` (1 Disruptor, 2 Flankers) and kept with `RoleStickiness`. Pressure prefers the front of the player (`PressureFrontWeight`), Flanker the sides and back (`FlankWeight`, bearing against the player's facing: 45° front and rear cones, `Swarm.Flank`), Reserve queues behind everyone for tokens, Opportunist jumps the queue while a window is open, Support is for healers. Disruptor is only possible for enemies with a telegraphed stun skill (`Swarm.Interrupts`: an enemy-targeted skill with a windup and a Stun-kind effect), because only stuns interrupt a player's cast; knockback only moves NPCs.
- **Director.** Each update reads the player: casting, animation lock or more than `Director.WindowGcd` seconds of GCD left opens a window (+`WindowBonus` melee and ranged tokens); below `MercyBelow` HP the budget drops by `MercyPenalty`, never below 1. Support is never changed.
- **Disruptor.** When its player starts a cast, the squad's Disruptor checks its interrupt skills: if walking into reach plus the windup plus `DisruptMargin` still beats the end of the cast (`Swarm.CanInterruptInTime`), it runs at the player (`Disrupt` state) and fires the skill without a token. A Disruptor keeps its interrupt skills out of its normal rotation so they are ready for the next cast. The windup broadcasts the usual telegraph. One try per player every `DisruptCooldown` seconds across all teams.
- **Morale.** `combat.morale = { base, fleeBelow }` (default `Tuning.Swarm.Morale.Default`) or `false` for enemies that never flee (undead, bosses, leaders). Damage costs `DamageWeight` morale per full max HP taken; an ally of the same team dying within `Radius` costs `AllyDeath`; a leader (`combat.leader`) dying within `LeaderRadius` costs `LeaderDeath`. Morale regenerates `Regen` per second up to base. A living leader of the team within `LeaderRadius` adds `LeaderBonus` and the `rally` status adds `RallyBonus` to the value compared with `fleeBelow`. Below it the enemy gives back its token and slot and either regroups behind the nearest leader (`Regroup`) or runs home (`Flee`); it fights again above `fleeBelow + Hysteresis`. Returning home resets morale.
- **Stagger.** Damage of `combat.poise` × max HP (default `Stagger.DefaultPoise` 0.2; brutes, soldiers and riders 0.3–0.35, the Captain 0.5, the Chieftain 0.6) taken within `Stagger.Window` seconds staggers an enemy: its windup or cast is cancelled, its token goes back, it plays the `Hit` cue and stops deciding for `Stagger.Seconds` (`Stagger` state). It cannot be staggered again for `Stagger.Cooldown` seconds.
- **Cost.** Thinks stay jittered per enemy and tactical updates are staggered per target, so 20 enemies per zone (`Tuning.Npc.ActivePerZone`) spread their work across frames.
