# AI and spawning

Code: `Server/Services/AIService.luau`, `SquadService.luau`, `SpawnService.luau`, `GroupService.luau`, `PopulationService.luau` (docs/systems/open-world.md), `Shared/Swarm.luau`, `Client/Controllers/EnemyAnimationController.luau`; template `src/ServerStorage/Templates/Goblin.rbxmx`.

Goblin template: the Goblin Scout's Studio import (`tools/world/goblin_scout_import.rbxm`, built by `tools/world/build.luau`): ten skinned MeshParts with PBR SurfaceAppearances on the bone tree of an invisible `Rig` part, jointed to a Humanoid's `HumanoidRootPart`, plus an invisible `Head` anchor for nameplates. Spawn scales it by `def.scale × BaseScale` (1.575); `Model:ScaleTo` scales HipHeight with it. The skinned mesh renders 1.75 studs below the import's RootPart, so the build raises the `Rig` by that much to put the soles on the ground. Spawn tints a template's `Body` part (the Skeleton); the Scout's textures carry its colours, so goblin `color` values no longer show. The model streams atomically.

Animation: AIService sets `AnimCue = "<clip>:<seq>"` on attack (melee, dash and self-area skills) or throw (everything else), hit, parry break (`Parried`, falling back to `Hit`) and death. Readability comes from those clips: basic enemy swings and throws whose set plays an Attack or Throw clip (Stab, Sling Shot, Poison Dart, Chieftain Cleave, Scratch, Claw, Sword Cut, Crossbow Bolt, Shield Strike) have `telegraph = false`, so they draw no ground marker, set no `Casting` attribute (no nameplate or target-frame windup) and no longer play the goblin bark, which rides the telegraph. Area attacks (Slam, Stomp, Poison Snare, Volley), casts (Hex Bolt, Curse) and Wolf Charge keep it. The client plays locomotion from root speed through `Shared/Locomotion`: planar speed is low-passed (0.15 s), walk enters above 0.8 and leaves below 0.3 studs/s, run enters above 9 and leaves below 7 (enemies move at 11 to 14), and a clip is held at least 0.25 s, so velocity jitter never restarts a clip; the playback rate follows the smoothed speed and is only reapplied when it changes by 0.05 or more, so hitstop holds on moving enemies. One-shots play from the cue. Clip ids come from `Assets.Animations`; every Goblin and Skeleton clip is uploaded, and an entry without an id would fall back to the KeyframeSequence in `ReplicatedStorage.Animations`, which only resolves in Studio.

- **Spawning** comes from the open-world population (docs/systems/open-world.md): `SpawnService.Spawn(npcId, level, position, { home, wander, hp })` builds a body (monster template, or a random R15 look for `Content/Npcs` people) and attaches a brain; `GroupService` decides when. Monsters are tagged `Enemy`, people `Npc`. Corpses go after 3 s.
- **Targets** are any entity of a hostile faction (`Shared/Relations`) found through a spatial grid rebuilt every step (`Shared/SpatialHash`, 32-stud cells); dummies are never targets. Monster factions never step into a safe zone or target anyone inside one; people move freely. Damage pulls in idle allies of the victim's faction (`Allied`) within 20 studs, so guards answer a villager or a caravan.
- **Tasks.** With no combat target a brain runs its task cascade (below). `AIService.SetGoal` gives the Patrol task a point to walk to (`Travel` state), `SetLeader` gives FollowLeader someone to trail, `SetHome` moves the leash and patrol centre and `SetSquad` groups brains that fight together; GroupService drives all four. `AIService.Busy` is true outside Idle, Patrol and Travel.
- **Brains** think every 0.25 s with jitter: Idle → Patrol (random point within `wander` of home) or Travel (to the goal), both at `Tuning.Npc.PatrolSpeedMultiplier` of walk speed (`StatService.SetPace`) → Aggro (taunt, then threat by the rules in [combat](combat.md), Threat, then the nearest player in aggro radius) → Position (waiting for an attack token) → Disrupt (running at a casting player) → Flee / Regroup (morale broken) → Stagger (poise broken) → Attack (first ready skill whose conditions hold and whose budget grants a token, tried in data order, or in a weighted random order when any of the enemy's skills sets `weight`, missing weights counting 1; `Swarm.WeightedOrder`) → Return (beyond the leash: clear threat, walk home, regenerate, reset). Players in safe zones are never targeted. Damage alerts nearby idle enemies of the same team.
- **Staying aggroed.** The chase radius is the definition's `leashRadius` × `Tuning.Npc.LeashMultiplier` (2; summons keep their exact leash), and a target stays valid `TargetSlack` studs past it. A target that drops out of play (out of range, shielded) is hunted at its last known spot for `LoseTargetGrace` seconds before the brain walks home. Morale and safe zones still end fights.
- **Behaviors** come from data: `keepDistance` makes ranged units keep their distance. After each attack they stand planted for `combat.plant` seconds (default `Tuning.Swarm.Ranged.Plant` 0.8; Slinger 0.8, Trapper 0.9, Archer 0.6, Hexer 1), facing the target, so a melee player can close in. Against a melee target (an NPC, or a player whose weapon's light attack is a melee swing) any move away from it is a retreat: allowed only inside `keepDistance`, with no attack ready, once per `RetreatCooldown` (3 s) and at most `RetreatDistance` (6 studs); otherwise they hold their ground. Against ranged players they still back off freely; Ally skills heal the most wounded ally; Dash skills open from range; group behaviour comes from `combat` (below). `EnemyDef.behavior` is kept only for special cases: `Dummy` never thinks, `Boss` labels an encounter boss (no AI branch). `combat.summons` spawns adds once HP falls below each entry's `belowHp`, applies its `selfEffects` and sets the `Phase` attribute (the Chieftain: two level-4 Raiders and Rally at 50%).
- **Movement** uses `Humanoid:MoveTo` on clear lines and `PathfindingService` (pcall, staggered recompute) when blocked.
- `AiState` is replicated as an attribute for the debug overlay. In Studio, brains also publish `AiRole`, `AiSlot` (ring, index and Front/Side/Rear), `AiToken` (`Held` or `Spent`) and `AiMorale` every think.

## Tasks

Pure logic: `Shared/Tasks.luau` (specs in `tests/specs/Tasks.spec.luau`). Tuning: `Tuning.Tasks`. Data: optional `tasks` and `stance` on an `EnemyDef`.

- **Cascade.** `tasks` lists what an NPC tries out of combat, highest priority first; every think it runs the first one whose precondition holds and falls through otherwise. Combat comes before all of them: the cascade only runs while the brain has no target, and the NPC returns to it after `Return`. Without `tasks` the list is `Tasks.Default`: FollowLeader, Patrol, Wander, Idle.

| Task | Possible when | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `FollowLeader` | It has a living leader within `LeaderRange` | Walks to its slot behind the leader |
| `Guard` (`tag`, `radius`) | Something tagged `tag` (alive, if an entity) is within `GuardRange` of home | Stays within `radius` (default `GuardRadius`) of it |
| `Patrol` | GroupService gave it a route goal | Walks to the goal |
| `Wander` | Its wander radius is above 0 | Random points around home |
| `Idle` | Always | Stands |

- **Following** (`Tuning.Tasks.Follow`). Followers form a loose clump: rows behind the leader's facing, `RowWidth` across and `Spacing` apart, shifted up to `Jitter` by noise seeded once from the member, so a slot never re-rolls. A follower keeps walking while its leader walks. Around a standing leader it stops within `ArriveRadius` and only starts again beyond `ResumeRadius`. Pace comes from the leader, through `StatService.SetPace`: its walking speed (full speed × `Tuning.Npc.PatrolSpeedMultiplier`) on the slot, × `SettleFactor` beyond `ArriveRadius` and × `CatchUpFactor` beyond `CatchUpDistance`, never above the follower's own speed. Separation (`Tuning.Swarm.Separation`) applies to where it walks.
- **Stance.** `stance` sets the attitude to players: `Hostile` attacks on sight, `Neutral` ignores them until attacked. Default by faction: monsters Hostile, everyone else Neutral. It does not change how factions treat each other: a Neutral soldier still attacks goblins on sight.
- **Attacking people.** Outside safe zones a player can attack any NPC (`CombatService.MayAttack`, used by `CanHarm` and every `SkillService` target check). NPCs in a safe zone only fight hostile factions. A player who hits a non-hostile NPC starts a grudge (`EntityService.Provoke`) that makes `AreEnemies` true both ways; a Hostile-stance NPC takes one when it sees a player. `startReturn` forgives. NPCs publish `Attackable` (outside a safe zone) and `Provoked` (fighting a player): the client's soft target takes only provoked people, so it never lands on a bystander, while a lock (Tab, tap) takes any attackable one. Melee and area hits land on any attackable NPC in reach, so a careless swing starts a fight.
- **Squads.** Brains with the same `SetSquad` key (a GroupService group) fight together, however far apart: when one is hit or picks a new target, the others gain threat on that enemy (and the grudge, if it is a player).
- **Safe zones.** No NPC targets a player inside a safe zone, except dev spawns: `/spawn` sets `Entity.ignoreSafeZone`, so that NPC and its summons enter, fight and can be hit in any safe zone, by its stance (a Neutral person still waits to be attacked). The command says so.

## Summons

Data: `Content/Summons.luau` (families), `Tuning.Summons`. Pure rule: `Shared/Summons.luau` (specs in `tests/specs/Summons.spec.luau`). Server: `SummonService`. Client: `SummonController`, the `SummonPicker` screen and the `SummonOption` template.

- **Options.** A skill with the `Summon` delivery names a family through `summon`. The caster may raise the family's `base`, plus each `pool` entry in the profile's `discoveries`. Create Undead: base Zombie; pool Zombie, Skeleton.
- **Discovery.** Getting kill credit on an enemy records its id in `discoveries` (`ProgressionService`). A kill that opens a summon option shows a toast. A summon's kill credits its owner (`CombatService` resolves it before `Died` fires), so XP, loot and discovery all follow.
- **Casting.** With one option the press casts it. With more, the press opens the picker with the last choice marked: clicking an option casts it and pressing the skill again casts the marked one. The combat cursor is free while it is open, and it closes after `PickerSeconds`. The choice travels as the fifth `UseSkill` argument, or after the name in chat (`Create Undead Skeleton`); the server refuses anything outside the options (reject reason `Summon`) before paying. A cast naming none uses the player's last choice this session, else the first option.
- **Limits.** One summon per cast at the caster's level + `levelOffset`, for `duration` seconds. A caster keeps at most the family's `cap`, and never more than `MaxPerCaster`; another cast replaces the oldest. Death, leaving or the end of the duration removes them; the last two use `SpawnService.Despawn`, so they leave no corpse.
- **Allegiance.** A summon spawns on the caster's faction with `entity.owner` set. `CombatService.MayAttack` only lets its real enemies hit it, so its owner, other players and their area attacks pass through it while hostile factions fight it on sight. It publishes no `Attackable`, so the client never aims at it. Ally skills can target it.
- **Behaviour.** It reuses the enemy brain. `FollowLeader` trails the owner at the owner's speed (`SetLeader`), `wander` is 0 and its stance is Neutral. Its home is the owner, so `Leash` is measured from them: past it, it drops its fight and walks back. Unlike other NPCs it does not heal on the way back or on arrival. It takes targets the usual way (threat, then nearest hostile within `aggroRadius`), and every hit the owner lands adds threat on the victim, with a grudge if their factions are not hostile. Hits on the owner already pull in nearby allies. A summon farther than `TeleportDistance` is brought back behind the owner.

## Group combat

One general system for every enemy, configured per enemy in `EnemyDef.combat` (`Content/Types`) with defaults in `Tuning.Swarm`. Pure logic lives in `Shared/Swarm.luau` (unit-tested under Lune); `SquadService` owns the live state; `AIService` asks it before acting.

- **Squads.** Enemies attacking the same player form that player's squad. The attack budget belongs to the player, so goblins and undead on one player share it without cooperating.
- **Attack tokens.** Each player target carries its own budget, `Tuning.Swarm.Budget` (2 Melee + 1 Ranged, plus 1 Support for ally skills), so the total grows with the number of players in a fight. A skill draws from the budget of its kind (`Swarm.TokenKind`: ally skills Support; Melee, Dash and SelfArea Melee; the rest Ranged), not from the enemy's slot, so an enemy with a heal and a strike uses both. `combat.tokenCost` (default 1, large enemies 2) is the share one attack takes. Slot `"None"` never waits (training dummy). A target that holds the pack, guarding or taunting a member of its squad, gets `Budget.TankBonus` (1) extra Melee tokens (`Swarm.HolderBonus`), so a tank can take the whole pack while a DPS keeps the base 2. Every attacker (Melee and Ranged slots, not fleeing) beyond the target's current Melee + Ranged budget makes it take `Overwhelm.PerEnemy` (3%) more damage, up to `Overwhelm.Max` (15%) (`Swarm.Overwhelm`, `Entity.overwhelm`, applied in `CombatService.DealDamage`), so a pack cannot be ignored by soaking hits.
- **Token lifecycle.** A melee enemy queues for a token as soon as an attack skill is ready, before walking into reach; without one it holds `WaitDistance` studs beyond its reach in the `Position` state, facing the target (ranged enemies wait at their own distance). Queues are per kind and FIFO by when waiting began, so the enemy that just attacked goes to the back. Spending a token sets its release to the end of the windup or cast plus `RecoveryHold`; an unused grant is taken back after `TokenTimeout`; a waiter that stops asking for `WaitStale` loses its place. Interrupts, death, leaving the target and returning home give the token back at once.
- **Engagement slots.** A tactical update per target, every `TacticInterval` (0.5 s) with a random start offset so targets do not update together, spreads attackers over two rings around the player: `Slots.Melee` (6) for melee enemies, `Slots.Ranged` (8) for everyone else. A slot is a bearing; each enemy stands on it at its own reach (melee: its shortest attack reach, capped at 8) (melee with a token at reach − 1, without one at reach + `WaitDistance`). `Swarm.AssignSlots` is greedy: it keeps taking the cheapest (enemy, free slot) pair, where cost is the walk to the slot plus `SlotSwitchCost` for leaving the current slot plus `SlotCongestion` for every taken neighbouring bearing in either ring. No slot is shared; enemies left over approach directly. Slots whose ray from the player hits a wall within the ring's widest radius are skipped.
- **Separation.** Enemies closer than `Separation.Radius` (4 studs) push each other apart, up to `Separation.Strength` studs added to the slot destination (`Swarm.Separation`).
- **Roles.** Each team on a target is its own squad. Every tactical update gives each member one role from its `combat.roles` weights (default `{ Pressure = 1 }`), capped per squad by `RoleCaps` (1 Disruptor, 2 Flankers) and kept with `RoleStickiness`. Pressure prefers the front of the player (`PressureFrontWeight`), Flanker the sides and back (`FlankWeight`, bearing against the player's facing: 45° front and rear cones, `Swarm.Flank`), Reserve queues behind everyone for tokens, Opportunist jumps the queue while a window is open, Support is for healers. Disruptor is only possible for enemies with a telegraphed stun skill (`Swarm.Interrupts`: an enemy-targeted skill with a windup and a Stun-kind effect), because only a stun interrupts a player's cast for sure (a hit interrupts only above `Tuning.Combat.Poise.CastPoise`, lighter hits push the cast back; [combat](combat.md), Cast); knockback only moves NPCs.
- **Director.** Each update reads the player: casting, animation lock or more than `Director.WindowGcd` seconds of GCD left opens a window (+`WindowBonus` melee and ranged tokens); below `MercyBelow` HP the budget drops by `MercyPenalty`, never below 1. Support is never changed.
- **Tempo.** `combat.tempo = { pressure, pause }` alternates seconds where the enemy may attack and seconds where it only repositions (ally skills still run), from a random offset per enemy so a pack does not pause together (`Swarm.TempoPhase`). A pause gives back an unused token. Raider 4/1.5, Skeleton 5/1, Zombie 6/1, Warband Soldier 5/1.5; without `tempo` an enemy attacks whenever it can.
- **Disruptor.** When its player starts a cast, the squad's Disruptor checks its interrupt skills: if walking into reach plus the windup plus `DisruptMargin` still beats the end of the cast (`Swarm.CanInterruptInTime`), it runs at the player (`Disrupt` state) and fires the skill without a token. A Disruptor keeps its interrupt skills out of its normal rotation so they are ready for the next cast. The windup broadcasts the usual telegraph. One try per player every `DisruptCooldown` seconds across all teams.
- **Morale.** `combat.morale = { base, fleeBelow }` (default `Tuning.Swarm.Morale.Default`) or `false` for enemies that never flee (undead, bosses, leaders). Damage costs `DamageWeight` morale per full max HP taken; an ally of the same team dying within `Radius` costs `AllyDeath`; a leader (`combat.leader`) dying within `LeaderRadius` costs `LeaderDeath`. Morale regenerates `Regen` per second up to base, but only once `RegenDelay` seconds pass without a new loss, so steady pressure keeps it down. A living leader of the team within `LeaderRadius` adds `LeaderBonus` and the `rally` status adds `RallyBonus` to the value compared with `fleeBelow`. Below it the enemy gives back its token and slot and either regroups behind the nearest leader (`Regroup`) or runs home (`Flee`; when home is within `FleeDistance` of the attacker it runs that far straight away from it instead); it fights again above `fleeBelow + Hysteresis`. Returning home resets morale.
- **Stagger.** An enemy's poise pool (`combat.poiseMax`, `combat.parryPoise`; rules in [combat](combat.md), Poise and parry) breaks under hits or parries: its windup or cast is cancelled, its token goes back and it stops deciding (`Stagger` state) for `Tuning.Swarm.Poise.Seconds`, or `Tuning.Parry.BreakSeconds` after a parry break. It cannot break again for `Tuning.Swarm.Poise.Cooldown` seconds. The pools are the old window shares times base MaxHp (brutes and soldiers 42, the rider 39, the Captain 300, the Chieftain 252; others the default 0.2 share), so stagger thresholds are unchanged.
- **Balance simulation.** `tests/Balance.luau` fights one level-3 Fighter (rusty sword, padded vest) against N same-level Goblin Raiders with the real content, tuning, tokens, tempo, poise and parry rules, abstracting space (token holders are in reach, swings hit one raider, nobody walks) and using fixed seeds. Personas: a DPS chaining sword slashes that parries 0, 1 in 4 or 1 in 2 attacks by dropping its swing, and a tank with the wooden shield that guards 1.5 s between single slashes (so it is a holder) and parries 1 in 4 from its guard. `lune run tools/sim/balance` prints win rates; `Balance.spec` asserts the tank target (5 to 8) and that parrying never lowers the DPS result. Results (2026-10-31, 40 seeds, largest pack won at least half the time): DPS 5, 6 and 7 by parry rate; tank 5. The DPS target (1 to 3) is not met.
- **Cost.** Thinks stay jittered per enemy and tactical updates are staggered per target, so the bodies near players (`Tuning.Population.MaxBodies`) spread their work across frames.
